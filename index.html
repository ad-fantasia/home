Thanks for visiting my corner. I am a PhD student working in theoretical physics at the École Normale Supérieure–PSL, Paris, working under the supervision of Professor Adam Nahum. I graduated with a Master's from the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai, in the Department of Theoretical Physics.

My primary interests lie in statistical and condensed matter physics, particularly phase transitions in various settings. Currently, I am working on measurement induced transitions in classical lattice systems and trying to understand the characteristics of the phase transitions better. At TIFR, I worked with Professor Kedar Damle on disordered systems and Ising models on hyperbolic lattices.
